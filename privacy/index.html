Privacy Policy

App: Code Route Algérien · Last updated: September 21, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the "Code Route Algérien" mobile application ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. The App helps users learn Algerian traffic signs, road rules, and traffic violations, with content available in Arabic, French, and English.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

The App itself does not require account creation and does not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

However, the App displays advertisements through Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service. To serve and measure ads, Google AdMob and its partners may automatically collect certain information, which can include:

  • Device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID)
  • Device type, operating system, and general technical information
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address)
  • App usage and interaction data related to ads (impressions, clicks)

This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with its own privacy policy, linked below.

How We Use Information

Any information collected through the App (via AdMob) is used solely to:

  • Display relevant advertisements
  • Measure ad performance
  • Protect against invalid traffic and fraudulent activity

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer any information to outside parties beyond what is necessary for the advertising services described above.

Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience learning traffic rules and is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can address it.

Data Retention & Security

We do not operate our own servers that store personal user data. Any data processed for advertising purposes is retained and secured according to Google AdMob's own data retention and security practices.

Your Choices

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device's ad settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads → "Opt out of Ads Personalization").
  • You can uninstall the App at any time to stop all data collection related to it.
